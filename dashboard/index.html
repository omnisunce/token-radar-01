How the radar scores a protocol

Implied value = TVL × peer multiple × growth × age × breadth. The peer multiple is the median market-cap-per-TVL of the protocols in the same category whose tokens already trade, shrunk toward the global median so a category with one traded peer cannot set a wild multiple. Growth is the 7-day TVL change, age is how long the protocol has run with no token, breadth is how many chains it runs on. Every number comes from the frozen DefiLlama snapshot dated — no names are hardcoded, and a protocol's rank is the model's claim, not a prediction that a token will launch, or when.

A protocol counts as farmable when the snapshot records no token for it and it is not a wrapped, staked or bridged product of a token that already trades. The watchlist is the 40 protocols at or above $50M TVL that meet that rule. The early finds are the farmable protocols below that floor — the radar exists to find them first.

Already launched — the traded peers behind the multiples

These tokens already trade, so they are not farmable. Their market caps are the yardstick: each category's multiple is the median of its peers' market-cap-per-TVL.
